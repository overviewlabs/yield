# Yield Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 2, 2026

Yield is a portfolio-analysis and automation-control application operated by Evans Obeng. This policy explains how Yield handles information when you use the application and related services.

## Information we collect

Yield may process:

- Account information, including your Sign in with Apple user identifier, name, email address, and Apple private relay address when you choose Hide My Email.
- Subscription information, including product identifiers, transaction identifiers, entitlement status, renewal status, and purchase history supplied by Apple. Yield does not receive your complete payment-card details.
- Financial and brokerage information made available after you authorize a supported brokerage connection, including masked account identifiers, account type, balances, holdings, transactions, orders, capabilities, and options-permission status.
- Preferences and instructions you provide, including risk settings, selected strategies, watchlists, notification choices, and automation controls.
- App activity and technical information needed to operate and secure the service, including feature interactions, device and session identifiers, push-notification tokens, integrity signals, timestamps, error information, and security/audit events.
- Information you provide in support requests.

## Brokerage credentials

Yield is not Robinhood and is not endorsed by Robinhood. When a supported brokerage connection is available, authentication occurs in the brokerage provider's authorized browser experience. Yield does not ask you to type your brokerage password into the Yield app. Provider authorization tokens are handled by restricted server infrastructure and are not stored in the iOS application.

## How we use information

We use information to authenticate users; provide portfolio monitoring, strategy analysis, risk controls, notifications, subscriptions, and support; maintain security and prevent abuse; reconcile brokerage and App Store records; comply with legal obligations; and improve reliability. Automated analysis can be incorrect and does not guarantee investment performance.

## How we disclose information

We may disclose information to service providers that support hosting, security, notifications, customer support, analytics necessary for app functionality, and automated analysis; to Apple for authentication and subscriptions; to a brokerage provider when you direct us to connect an account or transmit an authorized request; and to authorities or other parties when required by law, needed to protect users or the service, or involved in a business transaction subject to appropriate safeguards.

We do not sell personal information. We do not use personal information for cross-app advertising tracking, and the iOS app does not request permission to track you for advertising.

## Data retention and deletion

We retain information only as reasonably necessary to operate and secure the service, resolve disputes, enforce agreements, and satisfy legal, financial, audit, and regulatory obligations. Retention periods vary by record type. You can request account deletion from **Settings → Delete Account** or contact us at [admin@overviewlabs.ai](mailto:admin@overviewlabs.ai). Deleting Yield does not close your brokerage account, liquidate positions, or cancel brokerage orders. Open or unresolved orders, fraud or security investigations, legal holds, and legally required record retention may delay or limit deletion. We will restrict retained information to the purposes requiring retention.

## Security

We use administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards designed to protect information, including access controls, encrypted transport, restricted credential storage, and security logging. No system is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## Children

Yield is not directed to children under 18, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Your choices and rights

You may review or change app preferences, disconnect supported integrations, restore or manage App Store subscriptions, request access or correction, and request deletion. Applicable law may provide additional privacy rights. To exercise a right, contact us using the address below. We may need to verify your identity before completing a request.

## International processing

Information may be processed in the United States and other locations where service providers operate, subject to applicable safeguards.

## Changes

We may update this policy to reflect changes to the service or law. We will publish the updated policy with a new effective date and provide additional notice when required.
