## Subscription support

Subscriptions and free trials are billed by Apple. You can manage or cancel a subscription from your Apple Account subscription settings. Cancel before the trial or billing period ends to prevent the next renewal charge. Deleting the Yield app does not cancel an App Store subscription.

## Brokerage safety

Yield is not Robinhood and is not endorsed by Robinhood. Yield support will never ask for your Robinhood password or two-factor authentication code. Disconnecting or deleting Yield does not close a brokerage account, liquidate positions, or necessarily cancel open brokerage orders.

## Account deletion

Use **Settings → Delete Account** in Yield or email [admin@overviewlabs.ai](mailto:admin@overviewlabs.ai). Account deletion may be delayed where open or unresolved orders, security investigations, legal holds, or legally required record retention apply.

For suspected unauthorized activity, contact your brokerage provider immediately and then notify Yield support.
